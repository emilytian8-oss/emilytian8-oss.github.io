A More Connected Way to Source PCB & PCBA

YudaPCB helps electronics businesses navigate PCB fabrication and PCBA assembly with one point of contact. We match each project with suitable manufacturing resources, coordinate technical communication and follow up on agreed requirements from quotation to delivery.

Who we are

YudaPCB is an independent PCB and PCBA manufacturing solutions partner based in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. Built on eight years of PCB sales experience and customer project coordination, we understand how important clear specifications, responsive communication and dependable follow-up are to electronics buyers and engineering teams.

We work with multiple manufacturing partners rather than relying on a single production source. This allows us to review project requirements and identify a suitable route based on board construction, technical needs, order quantity and schedule. Manufacturing is carried out by the selected partner facility; YudaPCB manages customer communication and project coordination.

What matters in manufacturing coordination

Good sourcing is not just about finding a price. It is about making sure the project requirements are understood and agreed before production starts.

Engineering & quality communication

We help collect and clarify the technical information needed for a manufacturing review, including:

  • Gerber data, stack-up and fabrication notes
  • Layer count, material, thickness, copper and surface finish
  • Impedance, via structures and special process requirements
  • BOM, Pick & Place data and assembly drawings for PCBA
  • Inspection, test documentation and acceptance criteria

Transparent project scope

Before an order is placed, we aim to make the key commercial and technical details clear:

  • Confirmed manufacturing specifications and quotation scope
  • Partner capability and material availability
  • Agreed inspection and test requirements
  • Production schedule and delivery arrangements
  • Issue communication and remedy terms for nonconforming orders
Inspection methods, certificates, test reports, special materials and production lead times depend on the selected partner and project scope. They are confirmed before order rather than assumed to be available for every design.
