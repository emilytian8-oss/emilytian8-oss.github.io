About YudaPCB

Yuda Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is an independent PCB and PCBA manufacturing solutions partner based in Dongguan, Guangdong Province, China. We connect customer projects with suitable manufacturing resources through a network of specialized partner facilities. Production is carried out by the selected facilities; YudaPCB coordinates communication and project follow-up.

With a background in PCB sales and customer coordination, we help overseas SMEs, R&D teams and design houses source suitable manufacturing solutions for prototypes and production orders.

Our role is to understand your technical and commercial requirements, identify suitable manufacturing routes, coordinate DFM feedback and follow up on agreed quality and delivery requirements. This gives customers one point of contact across PCB fabrication and PCBA assembly, while production is completed by the selected manufacturing partners.

We See What Others Ignore

Reliability starts from inside each PCB. Selected manufacturing partners may provide micro-section and SEM analysis to verify copper plating thickness, hole wall integrity and layer alignment, especially for high-reliability and high-density designs.

Precision Inspection & Testing

Inspection methods such as Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) and electrical testing (E-test) are coordinated with the selected manufacturing partner according to the agreed specification. Our goal is to catch defects such as opens, shorts, missing pads or solder mask issues before the boards reach your assembly line.

Professional Packaging & Logistics

Oxidation and transit damage are two major risks during long-distance shipping. To protect your boards, we coordinate suitable moisture-protective packaging and ESD-safe cushioning with the selected manufacturing partner.

Our Commitment

DFM as Standard We coordinate a manufacturability review of Gerber files, including annular ring, trace-to-edge clearance, via structures and impedance considerations.
Transparent Supply Chain We work with multiple manufacturing partners and communicate agreed requirements, production progress and issues transparently.
Full Traceability Where available and agreed before production, we coordinate test reports, AOI records, E-test results and packaging photos from the manufacturing partner.

Specifications, inspection criteria and remedies are agreed before production.

  • ✓ Issue Review: We will coordinate a documented review with the manufacturing partner.
  • ✓ Agreed Remedy: Any rework, replacement or credit will be handled according to the confirmed order terms.
  • ✓ Communication: We will keep you informed during the resolution process.
