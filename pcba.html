PCBA Assembly Coordinated Around Your PCB Project

From bare PCB sourcing to assembled boards, we coordinate suitable assembly partners and help align BOM, components, process requirements, inspection scope and delivery.

One contact for PCB and PCBA sourcing

YudaPCB is a trading and sourcing partner. PCBA production is performed by selected partner facilities, not by an in-house assembly line. Service scope and feasibility are reviewed for each project before quotation.

01 · Engineering input

BOM & File Review

We collect BOM, Gerber, centroid / pick-and-place files, assembly drawings and special process requirements for partner review.

02 · Procurement

Component Sourcing

Component procurement can be coordinated when the BOM, approved alternatives, brand restrictions and supply conditions are confirmed.

03 · Assembly

SMT & Through-Hole

SMT and through-hole assembly options may be arranged through partner factories, subject to component package, board design and production capacity.

04 · Quality

Inspection & Testing

Visual inspection, AOI, X-ray, functional testing and other checks are discussed according to the product and agreed order requirements.

05 · Integration

PCB + PCBA

We can coordinate bare-board fabrication and assembly as one project, helping reduce communication gaps between separate suppliers.

06 · Delivery

Project Follow-up

We follow up on confirmed milestones, packing requirements and shipment arrangements with the selected manufacturing partners.

What to send for a PCBA quotation

The more complete the input, the easier it is to review feasibility and prepare an accurate quotation.

  • BOM with manufacturer part numbers, quantities and approved alternatives
  • Gerber files and PCB fabrication specifications
  • Pick-and-place / centroid file and assembly drawings
  • Special instructions, test requirements and target quantity
  • Target delivery date and shipping destination
