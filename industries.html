PCB & PCBA Solutions Across Electronic Industries

YudaPCB supports companies developing and manufacturing electronic products. We coordinate PCB fabrication, component sourcing and PCBA assembly through manufacturing partners selected according to each project's technical requirements, quantity and schedule.

Manufacturing support shaped around your application

Different industries have different priorities: electrical performance, reliability, board construction, assembly requirements, traceability or production flexibility. Share your design files and project needs with us. We will review the requirements and coordinate a suitable manufacturing route with the relevant partner facility. Actual process capability, certifications, inspection scope and lead time are confirmed for each project before order.

01 · Industrial electronics

Industrial Control & Automation

PCB sourcing support for electronic systems used in industrial equipment, automation and control applications.

Typical applications

  • Controller and interface boards
  • PLC-related electronics and I/O modules
  • Sensor interfaces and machine control
  • Industrial monitoring equipment

Project considerations

Board reliability, operating environment, electrical requirements, component availability and repeat-build consistency.

02 · Power electronics

Power Supply & Energy Electronics

PCB and assembly coordination for products that manage, convert or distribute electrical power.

Typical applications

  • AC/DC and DC/DC power supplies
  • Inverter and motor-control electronics
  • LED driver and lighting control boards
  • Battery and power-management systems

Project considerations

Copper weight, thermal management, creepage and clearance, materials and assembly requirements as specified by the design.

03 · Test equipment

Test & Measurement

PCB sourcing for test instruments, production test systems and electronic measurement equipment.

What to send for a project review

The more complete the information, the faster we can clarify feasibility and prepare a useful quotation.

For PCB fabrication

Gerber files, stack-up or layer count, material, thickness, copper weight, surface finish, quantity and target date.

For PCBA assembly

Gerber files, BOM, Pick & Place files, assembly quantity, component sourcing preference and testing requirements.

For early-stage projects

Product description, available drawings or design files, estimated quantity, development stage and questions for engineering review.
