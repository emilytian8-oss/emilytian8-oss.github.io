PCB Types for Your Next Electronics Project

From standard multilayer boards to advanced interconnect and special-material designs, YudaPCB helps match each project with a suitable manufacturing partner. Share your design files and requirements for a project-specific review.

Explore our PCB product categories

Choose a board type based on your electrical design, mechanical constraints, operating environment and production plan. If you are unsure which construction is appropriate, send us your files and application details. We can coordinate a review before quotation.

Important: The categories below describe project types we can evaluate through our manufacturing partner network. Material availability, stack-up, minimum feature sizes, tolerances, testing and production lead time vary by design and partner. Final capability is confirmed after engineering review; manufacturing is performed by the selected partner facility.
01 · General-purpose and complex boards

Multilayer PCB

Multi-layer FR-4 circuit boards for designs that need more routing density, controlled electrical performance or compact board layouts.

Common project details

  • Layer count and proposed stack-up
  • Board thickness and finished copper weight
  • Base material and Tg requirement
  • Surface finish, solder mask and board outline
Typical applications: industrial controllers, instrumentation, communication equipment and power control boards.
02 · High-density interconnect

HDI PCB

High-density interconnect constructions for compact layouts, fine-pitch components and designs with demanding routing density.

Common project details

  • HDI build-up structure, such as 1+N+1 or other designs
  • Microvia size, depth and via arrangement
  • Via-in-pad and filling requirements
  • Fine-line geometry and BGA pitch
Typical applications: compact embedded systems, portable electronics, dense control modules and communication devices.
03 · RF and microwave

High-Frequency PCB

RF and microwave board projects using suitable low-loss dielectric materials, subject to material selection and engineering review.

Common project details

  • Required dielectric material and Dk/Df values
  • Operating frequency and impedance targets
  • Stack-up, copper profile and surface finish
  • Material availability and bonding method
Typical applications: RF modules, antennas, wireless equipment and high-frequency signal paths.
04 · Flexible interconnect design

Rigid-Flex PCB

Combined rigid and flexible circuit constructions for products with limited space, shaped assemblies or fewer cable connections.

Common project details

  • Rigid and flex layer structure
  • Flex material, coverlay and stiffener details
  • Bend radius and static or dynamic flexing
  • Mechanical drawing and assembly constraints
Typical applications: compact instruments, medical electronics, industrial modules and space-constrained devices.
05 · Advanced via structures

Blind & Buried Via PCB

Multi-layer boards using blind or buried vias to improve routing density and support complex interconnections.

Common project details

  • Layer pairs and via structure
  • Blind / buried via dimensions
  • Stacked or staggered microvia design, if applicable
  • Via filling, capping and reliability requirements
Typical applications: dense BGA boards, advanced industrial electronics and compact high-layer-count designs.
06 · Signal integrity

Impedance-Controlled PCB

Boards designed around specified single-ended or differential impedance targets for high-speed and RF signal paths.

Common project details

  • Target impedance and tolerance
  • Signal layer and reference plane arrangement
  • Stack-up and trace geometry requirements
  • Impedance coupon and test report requirements
Typical applications: high-speed digital interfaces, communication hardware, test equipment and RF products.
07 · Special material combinations

Hybrid Lamination PCB

Multi-material constructions combining different laminate types when a design needs a balance of electrical, thermal or cost considerations.

Common project details

  • Material families and layer allocation
  • Thermal and electrical design requirements
  • Bonding compatibility and stack-up
  • Special copper or metal-core requirements, if applicable
Typical applications: RF and digital hybrid designs, power electronics and products with special thermal needs.
